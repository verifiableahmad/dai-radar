### Related, not yet tied to a tracked system

- **Ziv Meets Sumcheck (arXiv 2611.29188):** zero-knowledge proofs of
  floating-point transformer inference that prove the correctly
  rounded matrix product with a sumcheck-style interactive proof,
  instead of proving an integer approximation; it reports proving the
  full GPT-2 small forward pass in 19 minutes on two cores. ZKML, but
  not EZKL's Halo2 lineage or any other tracked system.
- **Beak (arXiv 2611.29260):** an RL-guided fuzzer for semantic gaps
  between RISC-V and zkVM constraint systems, reporting 25 of 37 known
  bugs found and 14 new bugs disclosed and patched across six
  production RISC-V zkVMs. The abstract does not name the zkVMs, so
  any link to SP1 or RISC Zero in systems/verified-zkevm.md is
  inferred only.
- **EVOLVE (arXiv 2611.28344):** a software proof-of-execution
  framework for ML workloads inside Intel TDX, built into the virtual
  firmware, with policy checks on primitives verified in F*. It extends
  TDX attestation from a launch snapshot to run-time behavior; no
  tracked system uses it.
- **HiPerfCC-KVCache and HiPerfCC-StateReuse (arXiv 2611.28702,
  2611.29067):** two papers that cut NVIDIA confidential GPU transfer
  costs in software (KV-cache transfer scheduling in SGLang, and reuse
  of exported ciphertext across transfers) on Hopper and Blackwell.
  They work around the CC-mode overhead that
  systems/nvidia-confidential-computing.md records; they do not
  re-measure it, so that row is unchanged.
