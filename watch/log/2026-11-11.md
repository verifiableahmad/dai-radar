### Related, not yet tied to a tracked system

- **zk-DEAP: a zero-knowledge decentralized encrypted aggregation
  protocol** (arXiv 2611.10020): lifted-ElGamal aggregation with FROST
  threshold decryption and per-input ZK range/constraint proofs
  (Bulletproofs, SNARKs or STARKs), plus a binding that stops a valid
  proof from being paired with a different ciphertext. Evaluated with
  5–500 participants. Field: federated learning / confidential
  aggregation. No tracked system uses it.
- **tritify: deterministic integer-arithmetic inference** (arXiv
  2611.13730): runs LLM, MoE and SAM 2.1 forward passes bit-identically
  across CPUs, GPUs and networked machines, including a 106B MoE split
  over three hosts. Field: optimistic on-chain AI verification, where
  re-execution needs determinism. The authors say a guarantee against a
  dishonest server is future work, so this is reproducibility, not a
  fraud proof.
- **zkARc: ZK receipts for neurosymbolic policy checks** (arXiv
  2611.10031): wraps each stage of an LLM-to-SMT policy-checking
  pipeline in a ZK proof, including model inference on Qwen2-0.5B. The
  authors report about an hour of CPU proving and under a minute to
  verify. Field: ZKML / verifiable inference. No tracked system uses
  it.
- **VeilTune: LoRA fine-tuning entirely in MPC** (arXiv 2611.11817):
  shows that existing secure fine-tuning leaks enough intermediate
  values to copy the adapter, then backpropagates entirely in MPC.
  Reported on RoBERTa-large few-shot tasks. Field: secure MPC for ML.
  It is a research protocol, not a change to Nillion's MPC layer, so
  systems/nillion.md is unchanged.
- **Doubly efficient interactive proofs over approximate arithmetic**
  (arXiv 2611.11618): extends GKR to layered circuits over the complex
  numbers where prover and verifier both use floating point, with
  closeness rather than exactness as the claim. Field: ZKML (sumcheck
  lineage). It is an interactive proof, not zero-knowledge, and no
  tracked system uses it.
- **Multiparty CKKS Newton-CG logistic regression** (IACR ePrint
  2026/2784): institutions aggregate encrypted gradient and Hessian
  statistics under multiparty CKKS and solve the Newton step by
  encrypted conjugate gradient, matching pooled plaintext training on
  clinical tasks. Field: FHE for ML. It is multi-key CKKS training,
  while systems/zama-concrete-ml.md covers TFHE inference on encrypted
  inputs, so that file is unchanged.
