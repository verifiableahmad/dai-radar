### Related, not yet tied to a tracked system

- **Ziv Meets Sumcheck (arXiv 2611.29188):** an interactive proof for
  correctly rounded floating-point matrix products, extended to every
  transformer layer, proving a full GPT-2 small forward pass in 19
  minutes on two cores with 21-second verification. Sumcheck-based ZKML
  in the line of foundations/zkml-sumcheck.md, but not tied to EZKL,
  DeepProve or another tracked system.
- **Beak (arXiv 2611.29260):** an obligation-guided fuzzer for RISC-V
  zkVM constraint systems that finds 25 of 37 known bugs across six
  production zkVMs and reports 14 new ones, all disclosed and patched.
  Relevant to the zkVM soundness question systems/verified-zkevm.md
  tracks, but it is testing, not formal verification, and the abstract
  does not name which zkVMs, so that file's Status row is unchanged.
- **EVOLVE (arXiv 2611.28344):** a software proof-of-execution layer in
  Intel TDX virtual firmware that binds an ML operation's execution
  status to measurements of its inputs and outputs, reporting 1.07x to
  2.13x per-request overhead. TEE-attestation work, not part of dstack
  or another tracked system.
