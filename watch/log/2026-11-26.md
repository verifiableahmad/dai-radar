### Related, not yet tied to a tracked system

- **Ziv Meets Sumcheck (arXiv 2611.29188):** a sumcheck-based ZK proof
  of correctly rounded floating-point matrix multiplication, extended to
  every transformer layer, proving GPT-2 small's full forward pass in 19
  minutes on two cores with 21-second verification. Sumcheck zkML, but
  not tied to DeepProve or another tracked prover.
- **FlashProve-Next (arXiv 2611.33741):** ZK-masked proofs for
  individual LLM API responses under native bf16/fp8 serving, using a
  Reed-Solomon weight commitment and spot-checking, with soundness
  mechanized in Lean 4. It reports proofs for Qwen3-0.6B and a
  relation on Qwen3-30B-A3B. zkML, research only.
- **EVOLVE (arXiv 2611.28344):** a software proof-of-execution layer in
  Intel TDX virtual firmware that binds an ML operation's run-time
  status to measurements of its inputs and outputs, with policy checks
  built on F*-verified primitives and reported overhead of 1.07× to
  2.13×. TEE remote attestation for ML, not a change to a tracked TEE
  stack.
- **Beak (arXiv 2611.29260):** an obligation-guided RL fuzzer for RISC-V
  zkVMs that finds 25 of 37 known bugs on six production zkVMs and 14
  new ones, all disclosed and patched (per the abstract; the zkVMs are
  not named there). Relevant to zkVM correctness, but fuzzing is not
  the formal verification that systems/verified-zkevm.md tracks.
- **SinCom (IACR 2026/3004):** shallow, wide sinusoidal composites for
  constant-round MPC evaluation of GELU, SiLU and similar activations.
  Field: secure MPC for ML. A research protocol, not a change to
  Nillion's MPC layer.
- **EASE-SVM (IACR 2026/3003):** two-party MPC training and
  RFF-based private inference for RBF kernel SVMs in medical IoT.
  Field: secure MPC for ML. No tracked system.
